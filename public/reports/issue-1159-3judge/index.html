i. The job

Open a trade. Open its counterweight.

In plain English: my bot needed to open a related counter-trade whenever a strategy asked for one. That counter-trade is a hedge. Either trade can complete only partly, fail, or be interrupted when the bot restarts. The bot then has to resize or close the other trade so the protection still matches.

ii. Blind first

Names came back after the scores.

In plain English: each pull request, meaning a proposed batch of code, became a letter from A through U. Judges could not see the model name, branch name, or author clues. They checked the real code, reran tests, and scored from fixed sheets. They never saw each other's numbers.

Fairness note, said out loud: every judge also had entries from its own model family. The final table flags those self-scores, and the winner gets a separate no-self check.

Two judges averaged near 88. One averaged 64.9.

In plain English: the average score from Fable and Grok landed near 88. Sol averaged about 65. Neutral checks found that many of Sol's harsh defect calls were real. They also found six tests Sol had missed.
